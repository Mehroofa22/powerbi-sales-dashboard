Power BI DAX Formulas Used in this Model Star Schema: FactSales ↔ DimDate, DimProduct, DimCustomer, DimRegion
Total Revenue =
SUMX(FactSales, FactSales[Quantity] * FactSales[UnitPrice] * (1 - FactSales[Discount]))
Total Profit =
[Total Revenue] - SUMX(FactSales, FactSales[Quantity] * FactSales[UnitCost])
Profit Margin % =
DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)
